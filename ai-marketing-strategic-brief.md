# VBO AI Marketing Landing Page — Strategic Brief

**Date:** May 1, 2026
**Author:** Atlas (Strategy)
**Page URL:** vboadv.com/ai-enabled-marketing

---

## 1. Positioning

**Core identity: "Miami's AI-Enabled Marketing Agency."** This is who VBO is. Lead with it confidently.

VBO occupies the **efficiency gap** — the space between two flawed models in a three-way positioning:

**Traditional agencies.** They have senior talent and real strategy — that's not the issue. The issue is their model requires too many people, too many layers, and too much time. You're paying for headcount and overhead, not better outcomes. The structure exists; it's just slow and expensive by modern standards.

**AI-first shops.** Fast and cheap, but shallow. No senior strategic thinking. Just pushing buttons and running prompts. Executional, not strategic. The client gets speed without direction.

**VBO is the unfair advantage.** Senior-level strategic thinking + AI-enabled execution. Human judgment driving AI efficiency. The brains without the bloat. You get the strategy of a traditional agency and the speed of an AI-first shop — without the trade-offs of either.

**Proprietary concepts:** "The Efficiency Gap" (the problem VBO solves) and "The Unfair Advantage" (VBO's position). Both should feel owned by VBO.

---

## 2. Tone & Voice

The VBO homepage establishes a clear voice: **confident, structured, no fluff.** Headlines are bold uppercase Barlow Condensed. Body copy is measured Inter. Mono-spaced accents (Space Mono) signal precision and system-thinking.

This landing page should match that voice with one adjustment: it should feel slightly more **diagnostic and editorial**. The homepage introduces VBO broadly. This page makes a specific argument — that most marketing is structurally inefficient, and VBO closes that gap.

**Tone markers:**
- Confident, not aggressive
- Calm authority, not hype
- Diagnostic, not salesy
- Structured, not trendy
- The language of a strategist briefing an executive, not a vendor pitching a prospect

**Avoid:** "AI-powered," "next-gen," "automation-driven," "cutting-edge," "operator," "leverage AI," "disrupt"

**Use:** "AI-enabled," "senior-level strategic thinking," "modern efficiency," "the unfair advantage," "the brains without the bloat," "human + AI," "efficient systems," "structured execution," "aligned marketing"

---

## 3. AI Identity Strategy

VBO is **"Miami's AI-Enabled Marketing Agency."** AI is not something to hedge about — it's part of the core identity. The word "AI-enabled" appears in the hero, the label, the meta title, and throughout the page. The key distinction is that VBO pairs AI with senior-level strategic thinking. AI without strategy is just automation. Strategy without AI is just slow.

**Approach:**
- "AI-enabled" is the modifier, not the headline — the headline story is always about the human strategic thinking that drives it
- AI appears confidently throughout: hero subhead, problem section, gap positioning, approach section, comparison table
- The three-way positioning makes VBO's AI stance clear: we're not AI-first (shallow), and we're not AI-avoidant (slow) — we're AI-enabled with senior strategy
- The frame is "Human + AI" — human judgment driving AI efficiency

**The frame:** AI is the engine. Senior strategic thinking is the driver. VBO is the vehicle that gives you both.

## 5. Target Audience

**Primary:** Business owners and marketing leaders (typically founder-led or growth-stage) who feel their current marketing is:
- Slow to execute and slow to adapt
- Expensive relative to results
- Fragmented across too many vendors, tools, and channels
- Lacking strategic direction — lots of activity, unclear ROI

**Secondary:** Marketing-aware executives evaluating agencies who have heard the AI pitch from multiple vendors and are skeptical of hype but curious about genuine efficiency.

**Psychographic:** These are people who value structure over flash. They want a partner who thinks before they spend. They resonate with diagnostic language, not aspirational language.

## 6. CTA Direction

The CTA should carry "modernize your marketing" energy — forward-looking, efficient, non-salesy.

**Primary CTA options (ranked):**
1. "Close the Gap" — proprietary, action-oriented, ties directly to the page concept
2. "Modernize Your Marketing" — clear, aspirational, implies leaving the old model behind
3. "Start With Structure" — ties to VBO's approach, implies the first step

**Avoid:** "Book a call," "Schedule a demo," "Get started free," "Talk to an expert"

**CTA destination:** mailto:hello@vboadv.com (consistent with main site) or link to contact/booking page if one exists.

## 7. Page Structure (Recommended)

1. **Hero** — "There's a Gap in Your Marketing." + efficiency framing

## 8. Design System Reference

Pull directly from the existing VBO site:

- **Background:** #0D0D0D (near-black), alternating sections with #1C1C1C (graphite)
- **Text:** #F2EDE4 (cream/off-white) for headlines and emphasis, #6B6F73 (steel grey) for body
- **Accent:** #B8962E (matte gold) for labels, accents, buttons, hover states
- **Headlines:** Barlow Condensed, 900 weight, uppercase
- **Body:** Inter, regular weight
- **Mono accents:** Space Mono for labels, tracking, small caps
- **Button style:** Outlined gold border, cream text, gold fill on hover with dark text
- **Section pattern:** Ghost section numbers (large, faint), gold vertical connector lines, section-label + section-accent pattern
- **Navigation:** Fixed top nav with logo, section links, "Let's Connect" CTA button
- **Footer:** Dark with logo, copyright, social icons, email
